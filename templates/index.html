About Me

Who I Am & What I Do

I'm an AI/ML Engineer and Full-Stack Developer from Nawabshah, Pakistan, currently pursuing Computer Systems Engineering at QUEST University. My primary focus is on Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning.

My journey in AI spans Data Science, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Generative AI. I've worked with Python, NumPy, Pandas, Matplotlib, and Seaborn; built supervised, unsupervised, and reinforcement learning models including Q-Learning, SARSA, and Deep Q-Networks; and explored deep learning architectures like ANN, RNN, CNN, and FNN in depth.

More recently, I've been diving deep into Generative AI — Transformers, Large Language Models (LLMs), RAG systems, and Agentic AI. Alongside AI, I build full-stack web applications with Flask, Supabase, and modern JavaScript.

I also have a strong foundation in core Computer Science — C, C++, OOPs, and Data Structures & Algorithms — from my university coursework.
